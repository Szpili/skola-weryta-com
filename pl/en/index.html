For schools and universities

Did you learn a lot and did you like it?

Two questions. One tap. That is all a student does after class.

Higher means: I learned more. Further right means: I liked it more. One tap never glues this into a single star — because a class can be hard and good at the same time.

How to use the square

Tap a cell. That is the only skill required.

What the school sees next

Every tap is one small colored square. Collect the whole class and you get a picture that reads in a second. Below are sample schools — unlabeled tiles, invented names, not real ratings. Each tile is a different school with its own staff. A school with no coat of arms gets a shield generated from its name. Full screen.

What this is not

This is not for holding teachers to account. A single bad vote means nothing. Votes are anonymous by design. The school sets the frequency. Full explainer — one page.
