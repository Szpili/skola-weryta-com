Skola — GirlTech USA × Weryta

Did you learn much, and did you like it?

Two questions. One tap. That is all a pupil does after a class.

The pupil taps one square. Higher up means: I learned more. Further right means: I liked it more. So one tap says two things at once, and the two never get squashed into a single score — because a class can be hard and still be good, and a class can be fun and teach you nothing.

One note on the learning axis. Dan’s API numbers its grid rows from the top, and records a tap on the top row — the row his own instructions call “I learned a lot” — as the value 0. Its learning field therefore runs opposite to its name, and we read it accordingly, so a teacher he describes as one whose students “really learned a lot” reads high here. On his screen the two inversions cancel and the picture is right; only the number is reversed. Colours are unaffected — they come from the columns. Flagged to Dan.
